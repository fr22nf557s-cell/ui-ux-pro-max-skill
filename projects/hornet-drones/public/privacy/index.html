Confirmation before anything else

We use double opt-in. After you submit the form we send one email asking you to confirm. Until you click that link, we send you nothing else, and if you never click it the record is deleted. That one confirmation email is the only message an unconfirmed address ever receives.

Cookies

We set no cookies. The site stores your campaign parameters in your browser's sessionStorage so that attribution survives you moving around the page; it is cleared when you close the tab and is never read by any third party.
