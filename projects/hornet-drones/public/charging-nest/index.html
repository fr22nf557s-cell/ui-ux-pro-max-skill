All-Weather Autonomous Charging Nest

A sealed, heated dock that opens on command, recharges to 80% in 22 minutes and rides out a storm at IP67. The drone launches, patrols and re-seats itself without anyone touching it.

  • 80% / 22 min
  • IP67 sealed
  • Self-seating

Why the nest matters

A security drone that needs a person to swap batteries is a toy. The nest is what turns the HRN-01 into something that patrols at 3 a.m. in the rain while you sleep, and is charged and ready when the next event happens.

Keys never leave it

The encrypted link between the nest, the aircraft and the command view is generated on the nest itself. There is no cloud account holding your keys, because there are no keys anywhere else. See the link specification.

Where it goes

Outdoors, on a level surface with clear sky above it, within reach of mains power. Placement guidance for your property is part of the alpha onboarding.
