Boilerplate

Hornet Drones is building an autonomous aerial security system for private property. The HRN-01 is a quiet perimeter drone with thermal, optical and low-light sensing that classifies what it sees on the aircraft itself and returns to a weatherproof nest to charge. The company is pre-launch, with a first alpha programme opening to its waitlist.
