Live feed

Switch between the optical and thermal cameras with one tap. The thermal view is what finds a person in a hedge at midnight; the optical view is what tells you it is the neighbour's cat.

Radar and contacts

Every moving body inside the perimeter appears as a contact, tagged with what the aircraft has classified it as. Known contacts stay quiet. An unclassified contact at a boundary is flagged, with its location.

Geofence

Draw the boundary of your property once, and adjust it by dragging its handles. A breach of that line is what raises an alert; a fox crossing the lawn is not.

Deploy Intercept

One control sends the aircraft to the flagged contact to put eyes on it. It arms, launches, and reports back over the same live feed. It observes and deters; it does not engage.

Event log

Every sweep, classification and breach is logged with a timestamp, and the log lives on your premises. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud account for you to read later.
